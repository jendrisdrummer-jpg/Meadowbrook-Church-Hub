# Leader feedback and "You said, we did"

The record of what staff and ministry leaders tell us, and what we did about it. Stage 2 is built from this list, not
from guesses. The process is in [master-product-roadmap.md](master-product-roadmap.md) §9.

## How feedback arrives
- **The hub's Feedback page** (sidebar → Admin → Feedback) and **the app's More → Send feedback**. These record the
  page, role and campus, and notify admins. Admins triage on the same page: status, an admin-only note, and "what
  we did", which feeds the "You said, we did" list in the hub.
- Messages in the "Hub feedback" chat, or a ministry's own feedback chat.
- The monthly huddle with each ministry's advisory group.
- **Blocks Sunday:** contact the named on-call person directly. Don't wait for triage.

The triage owner copies each item here within a day, so nothing lives only in a chat.

## Categories and severity
- **Category:**
  - **Bug:** something works wrongly.
  - **Frustration:** it works, but it's hard or slow.
  - **Missing:** something they do in Faith Teams, Google Chat or Subsplash can't be done here.
  - **Workflow:** an improvement to how something is done.
  - **Request:** a new feature.
  - **Ministry idea:** an idea for one ministry.
- **Severity:**
  - **Blocks Sunday:** fixed the same day, or the ministry falls back to its old tool.
  - **Slows me down:** fixed in the next batch.
  - **Nice to have:** goes to the backlog.
- **Status:** Received → Looking → Planned → Done, or Not now (always with a reason).

## Prioritised feedback list

- blocks a ministry leaving Faith Teams
- has several requesters
- affects safety, security or reliability
- is cheap
