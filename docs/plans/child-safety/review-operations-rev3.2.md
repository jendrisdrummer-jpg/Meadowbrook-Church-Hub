# Church Operations review of revision 3.2 (owner questions Q1–Q3)

Church Operations · 2026-11-25 · Sunday-morning practicality review of the revision 3.2 draft and the operational
policy.

**Verdict: workable with changes.** All changes below are applied in [revision-3.2.md](revision-3.2.md) unless
marked as an owner to-do.

## Q1. Temporary pickup procedure
- **Children too young to be named:** "the adult says each name" fails for infants. The room leader confirms the
  child knows the adult.
- **Busy pickup:** at a busy pickup, a second family on the screen is easy to miss. The rule needs to be physical:
  - Never press "Check out all"; tick the named children one by one.
  - Read the child count aloud against the tag.
- **"Known to the volunteer":** this is a loophole. "Known" must mean on the household or allowed list on the screen.
  Everyone else shows ID.
- **The parent phone call:** it is the choke point. It needs:
  - a named duty coordinator, with a number every room can reach
  - the child waiting in the room, not at the door
- **A room with only one adult:** say what happens.
- **The paper pickup-alert list:**
  - It should name the restricted adult (with a description), not only the child.
  - It needs a handover between services and warnings to rooms holding a listed child.
  - Name who prints and who shreds it, and set out a lost-list procedure.
  - State its limit honestly.
- **Missing rules:** what to do when a parent refuses ID, and the phone policy (whose phone; fetching a parent from
  the service).

## Q2. Approved release roster
- **Realistic:** Tier B is a handful of releases per service.
- **Design for failures:** dead phones and poor signal in basements. Add:
  - a "waiting for approval" screen with the time so far
  - a code-entry alternative for a late push
  - "approved by X at hh:mm" shown on the iPad
- **Size:** expect 8–15 roster people per campus, 3–5 present per service. Renewals need an owner.
- **The screening tick:** say who may tick it. An admin can't tick it on their own grant.

## Q3. Labels
- The last-name initial is fine.
- **Clash rule:** check it against every child eligible for the room, so a later arrival can't make an earlier label
  ambiguous. Twins with the same name need a tie-breaker (age).
- **Allergen words:** never cut them short. Parents' free-text allergies need mapping to allergen words.
- The interim full medical note with the full last name: drop the last name in P0.5, or accept the risk. **Applied:**
  P0.5 drops it.
