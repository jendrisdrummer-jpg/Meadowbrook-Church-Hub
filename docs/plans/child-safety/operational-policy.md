# Kids' check-in: how we keep children safe at pickup

*Operational policy for staff, coordinators and volunteers. **DRAFT, revision 3,** for the owner's approval.*

Words in [brackets] are settings the church chooses. Sections marked **PENDING REVIEW** must be checked by the
church's insurer and attorney before they take effect.

---

## 1. The basics
- **Every child gets a label, and every family gets a pickup tag.**
  - Both show the same code, the date, the check-in time and the children's initials.
  - The code is 5 or 6 characters, for example `K 7Q3M`.
- **A code finds the family. It never, on its own, lets anyone take a child.**
  - The volunteer always confirms who is collecting.
- **If anything looks wrong, stop and call a coordinator.** You will never be in trouble for asking.
- **Never say a child's name first.** The adult tells you which children they are collecting.
- Each iPad has its own letters, so codes don't repeat. If an iPad's memory was ever copied or damaged and a code
  repeats, the server notices as soon as the iPads are online.
- At any time, a code only finds a family. You still check that the person collecting belongs to that family, by
  their photo or ID, before anyone leaves.
- If the iPad shows two families for one code, check ID and call a coordinator.

---

## 2. Who's on the desk
- At the start of your shift, tap **"Who's on this desk?"** and choose your name.
  - Choose "Change person" when you hand over.
  - The iPad asks again after 20 minutes of no use.
- **Adults (18 and over)** can do every desk task.
- **Volunteers under 18** can check children in. They can release a child **only** to a parent or listed adult whose
  photo matches.
  - For anything else, press **"Get an adult helper"**.
- If your name isn't on the list, or the iPad says you're under 18 when you're not, ask the office to add or correct
  your birthdate.

---

## 3. Pickup: the two lanes
The iPad decides the lane. You don't choose it.

### Green lane (most pickups)
1. Type the code from the parent's tag. The family's children appear.
2. Check each child's label against the tag: the initials and the time. Untick any child who is staying.
3. Ask **"Who's collecting today?"** Let them tell you their name. Tap their photo, and check it's the same person.
4. Tap **Release**.

| Situation | What you do | Who can do it |
|---|---|---|
| Parent or listed adult, photo matches | Release | Any trained volunteer |
| Listed adult with no photo on file | Look at their ID, tick "ID checked" (and "Remember" if offered), release | Adult volunteer |
| Someone holding the tag who **isn't on the list** | Follow the **tag-holder steps** (§4) | Adult volunteer |
| Parent with **no tag** (lost) | Follow the **lost tag steps** (§5) | Adult volunteer |
| ID doesn't match, or anything feels wrong | Press **"ID doesn't match / call coordinator"** | Coordinator |

**When the iPad is offline:** tag-holder pickups and lost-tag pickups need a coordinator at the desk, who types
their offline code (§10). Everything else in the green lane works as normal.

### Red lane (careful)
The iPad shows a **red banner**. This happens when:
- a child has a **pickup alert**,
- a code matches **two families**, or
- something else is unusual.

What to do:
- Children start unticked.
- Ask who is collecting **first**.
- **Always check ID.**
- Follow the screen.
- Most red-lane pickups need a coordinator's approval.
- **A child with a pickup alert can only be approved from the coordinator's own phone.** A PIN or an offline code
  won't work for them.

If a code matches two families:
- Say: *"Two tags have the same code. I'll just check your child's name and your ID. Thank you for waiting."*
- The adult must name their children. Their ID must match one family.
- A coordinator approves.

---

## 4. Someone holding the tag who isn't on the family's list
Grandparents, babysitters and friends often collect children. We allow it when **all** of these are true:
1. The child has **no pickup alert**.
2. The family has **not** chosen "Only people on our list may collect" (§6).
3. **They hand you the physical printed tag. You keep it.** A photo of a tag, a screenshot or a written code is not
   accepted.
4. **You are an adult volunteer (18 or over).**
5. **The person collecting is at least [16]**. Check the date of birth on their ID.
6. **They name each child before you show them.**
   - For babies, and children who can't say whether they know the person, the room leader watches that the child
     knows them.
7. **You look at their ID.** Record:
   - the name as printed
   - the type of ID (driver's licence, passport, state ID, other)
   - their relationship to the child

   **Never write down an ID number.**

**If the iPad is offline:** a coordinator must come to the desk and approve with their offline code. Don't release
until they do.

What happens next:
- The coordinator on duty is told **immediately**.
- The child's parents who have the church app get a message straight away. It says who collected the child and when,
  and gives the coordinator's number.
- A coordinator checks every tag-holder pickup **by the end of the day**.
- If any check fails, press **"Call coordinator"**. Keep the child with the room leader.

---

## 5. Lost tag
**A parent or listed adult already known to us:**
1. Tap **"No tag?"** Search by **their** name or phone number (not the child's name).
2. They name their children.
3. Check their photo or their ID.
4. Choose a reason, then release.
5. The old code stops working. Any children still in rooms get a new tag.

An adult volunteer can do this **while the iPad is online**. **Offline,** a coordinator must approve with their
offline code, because the other iPads can't yet know the old code has stopped working.

**Anyone else without a tag, or the ID doesn't match:** call a coordinator.

---

## 6. "Only people on our list may collect" (a family's choice)
Parents can ask the office to turn this on for their family.

| | Parents and listed adults | Anyone else, even holding the tag |
|---|---|---|
| **Standard "list only" setting** | Green lane as normal | Coordinator only. The coordinator **must speak to a parent by phone** first. If no parent can be reached, **the child stays** with the room leader until a parent comes or answers. |
| **"Strict" setting** (the family's choice) | Green lane as normal | **Never released**, even by a coordinator. A parent must come, or add the person through the office first. |

- A parent at check-in can add a one-time pickup person for that day. That person then counts as on the list for the
  day, and is always ID-checked.
- When the iPad is offline, a "list only" release to someone not on the list waits for a coordinator with a phone
  signal.

---

## 7. Pickup alerts and the hard block
- **"Do not release to this person"** is a hard stop. Nobody can override it: not a coordinator, not the pastor, not
  an admin.
  - The church's emergency switch for faulty alert records never lifts a "do not release". It needs two people, and
    it lasts at most 4 hours.
- **"Check with a coordinator first"** means a coordinator must approve every release of that child.
- Alerts appear on the iPad, never on labels or tags.

**If the person named in an alert arrives**, the screen tells you what to do. Follow it calmly:
1. **Do not release.** Keep the child with you or with the room leader.
2. Press **"Alert coordinator now"**.
3. Say calmly: *"I need my coordinator for this pickup. Please wait here with me."* Don't explain why. Don't argue.
4. If anyone is threatened, call **911**.
5. Afterwards, the coordinator writes an incident note. The pastor is told.

## 8. ID checks
**We look at ID for:**
- anyone without a photo on file
- an adult newly added to a family
- one-time pickup people
- tag holders
- lost tags
- any child with a pickup alert
- code mix-ups

**What to say:** *"We check ID for anyone we don't know. It keeps every child safe. Thank you!"*

**How to check:** the face matches, the name matches, and the person is at least [16] where it matters. Record the
ID **type**, never the number.

## 9. Medical information: who sees what
| Level | What it is | Who sees it |
|---|---|---|
| **1 · Safety line** | Allergy, EpiPen yes or no, one line of what to do, where the medicine is | **Everyone serving children, including volunteers under 18.** Printed on the child's label as the allergy strip and "EPI: YES". |
| **2 · Full care note** | The full care information from the parent | **Adult volunteers (18 and over), coordinators, staff and admins**, on the Rooms screen, **only for children checked in today**, by tapping "Show care note". The note comes from the server when you tap, so it needs a connection. When the iPad is offline, the Level 1 safety line is still shown. |
| **3 · Private notes** | Sensitive information that isn't needed for care in the room | **Coordinators and admins only**, in the hub. Never on an iPad. |

- **Every time someone opens a full care note or a private note, the server records it.** The safeguarding lead
  reviews the record monthly.
- **Offline:** the safety line (allergy, EpiPen, what to do, where the medicine is) is always on the iPad and on the
  label. The full note returns when the connection does.
- Medical details are for caring for the child only. Never repeat them.
- For a few children with life-critical plans (EpiPen, seizure plan), the full note is printed on the label. This is
  re-confirmed with the parent each term.
- **In an emergency:** follow the safety line, call 911, call the coordinator, call the parent.

## 10. Coordinators: approving, and the offline code
- **Children with a pickup alert:** approve **only from your own phone** (hub app or Live page). A PIN or an offline
  code won't work for them.
- **Other children, online:** approve from your phone, or type your **personal PIN** on the iPad.
  - The PIN is 8 digits, made by the hub, not by you. It changes every 90 days.
  - It works only while the iPad is online. Cover the keypad as you type; the screen shows no digits.
  - **Every time your PIN is used, you get a message.** If you didn't approve it, tap "Not you?". The PIN is changed
    at once.
  - **Never** sign in to the hub on a check-in iPad.
- **Offline (church Wi-Fi down):** use **today's offline code**.
  - **How to get it:** in the hub or app, open **"Today's offline code"** before the service, while you're online.
    It's ready from 6 pm the evening before. You get one only if you're on the duty rota for that campus and day.
  - It is **10 digits**. It works **only at that campus, only that day, only on check-in iPads, only while they are
    offline**.
  - It can approve only these, for children **with no pickup alert**: lost tags, tag-holder pickups, code mix-ups,
    and pickups when the iPad's safety list is out of date. For anything else, wait for a phone signal.
  - **To use it:** tap your name on the iPad, then type the code. After 5 wrong tries, you are locked out on that
    iPad for 15 minutes. Other coordinators can still approve.
  - **Never share it.** No texts, emails, photos or chat. You may write it on a card for that day only, then destroy
    the card.
  - Every offline approval is checked by the server when the iPad reconnects, and **you are told about each one**.
    If you get a message about an approval you didn't make, report it straight away.
  - **If you think your code was seen or lost:** tap "Rotate my offline code" in the app (when online), or tell an
    admin. Codes also change automatically every day.
- **You may never approve your own request, or a release to yourself or your own family.**
- Ending or weakening a pickup alert needs a **different** coordinator or admin to confirm. It's never confirmed by
  the person who asked for the end, or by anyone in the child's family.

## 11. Lost or stolen iPad: within 15 minutes
| When | Who | What |
|---|---|---|
| At once | Anyone | Tell a coordinator. |
| Within 2 minutes | Coordinator or staff | Hub → Check-in → Stations → **Revoke**, reason "Lost / stolen". This also changes today's offline codes at that campus automatically. Coordinators then open the app for their new code. Anything the lost iPad sends afterwards is held for a coordinator to review; it is never applied automatically. |
| Within 5 minutes | Admin | Lock and erase the iPad (Lost Mode / Erase in the device manager, or Find My). |
| Within 10 minutes | Coordinator | Open the iPad's "since last sync" report. Count the children in each room against the open check-ins. |
| Within 15 minutes | Admin | Log an incident. Until the new medical levels are in place, the iPad held every listed family's medical notes and phone numbers. Afterwards it holds children's names, allergies and safety lines, and the last 4 digits of phone numbers, but no full care notes. Ask the insurer or attorney whether families must be told. |

**Device rules:**
- Check-in iPads are locked to the check-in app (Guided Access or Single App Mode) and have a passcode.
- No personal Apple ID, and no iCloud backup.
- Automatic date and time stay on.
- iPads are opened only from their Home Screen icon.

## 12. When the system is down: paper fallback
Use the paper kit when:
- an iPad says *"This iPad can't check in until it has been online"*,
- printing fails, or
- nothing works.

**Kit (in the desk folder):**
- numbered sign-in sheets for each room
- **pre-numbered tag pairs**, each with this desk's letter
- the daily pickup-alerts sheet

**Check-in:**
1. Write the child, parent, phone, allergy, room and time on the sheet.
2. Stick one half of the tag pair on the child, and give the other half to the parent.

**Pickup (the same rules as on the iPad):**
1. A matching number only finds the child. It is **never enough on its own**.
2. Ask **"Who's collecting today?"** first. The adult names the children before you say any names.
3. **Keep the tag half** they hand you.
4. **Check the pickup-alerts sheet for every child.** If there's an alert, call a coordinator.
5. Only the parent or adult **written on the sheet at check-in** may collect without a coordinator (the paper kit
   has no family lists). Check ID if you don't know them. Families marked "list only" on the pickup-alerts sheet
   **always** need a coordinator.
6. **Anyone else is a tag holder.** An adult volunteer writes the ID name and type (never the number) and the
   relationship on the sheet, and **calls the coordinator before releasing**.
7. **Volunteers under 18** release only to the parent written on the sheet. Otherwise, get an adult helper.

A named person enters the paper records into the hub on Monday.

## 13. Before every service: checklist
- [ ] Every iPad is charged and opened from its Home Screen icon. The header shows "Online", the current version and
      the "Safety list" time.
- [ ] Hub Stations page: no "Needs reload" or "Not moved" warnings.
- [ ] Check-in is open for this service.
- [ ] Label rolls and printers are ready. The parent tag prints every time, so expect double the labels.
- [ ] The pickup-alerts sheet is printed and in the coordinator folder. It is shredded after the service.
- [ ] The duty rota has two approving coordinators and one safety runner who is not teaching. The coordinators have
      today's offline code. Each iPad shows "Offline approvals ready".
- [ ] The paper kit is in the folder.

## 14. Records we keep (retention summary)
**PENDING REVIEW by the church's insurer and attorney. Nothing is deleted automatically until they have reviewed this
and the owner approves each step.**

- **Kept long-term:** who was checked in and out, who collected each child (with the ID type, never the number),
  pickup alerts, incidents and who was serving.
  - Allegations can come to light years later. These records protect children and volunteers.
- **Removed after a while (proposed):**
  - one-time pickup people's phone numbers: after 90 days
  - photos of pickup contacts: 90 days after they stop being a contact
  - medical notes of children who have not attended for 24 months
  - security logs: after 2 years
- **Never deleted:** check-in and serving history. The system refuses to delete people or services that have
  history.
- **Legal hold:** if an allegation, claim or subpoena arrives, an admin **sets a hold first, before anything else**.
  - While a hold is on, nothing is removed, and no backup copy in Google Drive is deleted.
  - Releasing a hold needs two different people. Old copies made during the hold are kept for 7 more days after
    that.
  - The Drive account owner must not delete backup copies by hand while a hold is on.
- **Corrections:** history can't be deleted or changed after the fact. Mistakes are marked "removed" or "cancelled",
  with who and why. Approved deletions need two people and keep a copy of what was deleted.
- **Who can see older records:** coordinators, admins and the safeguarding lead. Exports need a reason and are
  recorded.
