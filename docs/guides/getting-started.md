# Getting started with the Church Hub

**Who it's for:** every staff member trying the hub. About 10 minutes.

## Sign in

1. Open the hub's web address (the office will send it to you) on a computer or phone.
2. **With Google:** tap **Sign in with Google** and pick your church Google account.
   (This works on the website. It isn't offered inside the iPhone app; see
   [The phone app](the-phone-app.md).)
3. **With your email, the first time:** tap **First time, or forgot your password? Email me a code**.
   Type your email and tap **Email me a code**.
4. Check your inbox for a 6-digit code (it can take a minute; check spam too). Type it in **Code**.
5. Under **Choose a password**, pick one with at least 8 characters, then tap **Continue**.
6. Next time, just type your **Email** and **Password** and tap **Sign in**. Forgot it? Use the
   same **Email me a code** link to choose a new one.

You stay signed in for about a month on each device.

## The Home page

You'll see a greeting with your name, then cards:

- **Needs your attention**: tiles such as open volunteer spots this week, people who haven't
  replied, and regulars who've missed a few Sundays. Tap a tile to go and deal with it.
- **This week's services**: each service with how full it is, and **Schedule** and **Attendance**
  buttons.
- **Your next time serving**: your own requests, with **Accept** and **Can't make it** right there.
- **Latest attendance**, **Away this week**, and a line of people totals at the bottom.

**Make Home yours:** tap **Customise** (top right of Home). Switch cards on or off, and use the up
and down arrows to change the order. Tap **Save**. **Reset to default** puts it back. Only you see
your layout. (In that list some cards have slightly different names: "Last Sunday's attendance" is
the **Latest attendance** card, and "Who's away" is **Away this week**.)

## Find things

- **On a computer:** the menu is down the left side, in groups (People, Services, Church app, More).
  **Help** (the ⓘ icon) and **Feedback** (the speech-bubble icon) sit beside your name at the bottom.
- **On a phone:** the bar along the bottom has **Home**, **Schedule**, **Chat** and **People**. Tap
  **More** for everything else, plus **Help**, **Feedback**, **Sign out** and light or dark mode.
- **Search:** the **Search** box at the top (or the magnifying glass on a phone) finds people, pages
  and more. On a computer, Ctrl K (⌘ K on a Mac) opens it.
- **Two campuses:** the **Campus** box at the top of the menu (inside **More** on a phone) picks
  which campus you're looking at, or **All campuses**.

## Notifications

1. Tap the bell at the top right. You'll see your recent notices. **Mark all read** clears them.
2. To get notices on your phone or computer, open **My Schedule** and tap **Turn on** beside
   **Notifications**, then allow them when your browser asks.
3. **Choose which notices you get** (on the same page) lets you switch kinds off, and turn
   **Also send me emails** on or off.

On an iPhone, notifications only work after you add the hub to your Home Screen (Share, then
**Add to Home Screen**) and open it from there.

## Help and feedback

- **Help** has short how-to guides for the things you can do.
- **Feedback** is where you tell us what's broken, slow or confusing. Choose **What kind** and
  **How much does it matter?**, write what happened, and tap **Send**. **You said, we did** shows
  what we've changed.
- If something stops you on a Sunday morning, also tell your contact in person.

## If something goes wrong

- **No code arrived:** wait a minute, check spam, then tap **Send a new code**.
- **"This email can't create an account here":** the office hasn't added you yet. Ask them.
- **A message says your account isn't connected to you:** the office is told automatically and will
  link it. You don't need to do anything.
- **A page you expect isn't in the menu:** your account may not have that access yet. Ask the
  office.
