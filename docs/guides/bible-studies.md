# Look after Bible studies

**Who it's for:** the Bible studies coordinator (an account an admin has given the **Bible studies
coordinator** switch), and staff. Works on a computer, a tablet or a phone. Everything saves as you go.

Bible studies are personal: mostly one person with a teacher, sometimes a few people. Nobody else
sees who is studying with whom, and there are no reminders for missed weeks.

## Open the page

1. Tap **Bible studies** in the menu (under **People**; in **More** on a phone).
2. The four tiles show **Active studies**, **Paused**, **Finished this year** and **Waiting for a
   match**. Tap one to jump to it.
3. The tabs are **Waiting**, **Studies**, **Teachers**, **Materials** and **People** (look someone up by name).

## Keep the approved list of materials

1. Open **Materials** and tap **Add a material**.
2. Type the title. The author, the number of lessons and notes for teachers are all optional.
3. To take one off the list, tap **Retire**. Studies already using it, and everything people have
   finished, keep it. You can **Bring back** a retired one any time.

## Match someone who asked for a study

When someone asks for a Bible study, you get a notice (it never includes their name) and they appear
on **Waiting**, oldest first. (The "I'd like to do a Bible study" button in the church app arrives in a
later release; until then, **Waiting** stays empty and you start studies yourself.)

1. Read what they'd like: one-on-one or a small group, a material or topic, good times.
2. Tap **Start a study**. Choose a material if you know it (or leave it for the teacher to choose),
   add a teacher with **Add a teacher**, and tap **Start**. The person is already the student.
   Or tap **Add to an existing study** to put them in a study that's running.
3. If the card says they haven't shared their contact details, make the introduction yourself. If
   they have, their teacher will see their phone and email.
4. Not going ahead? Tap **Close** and pick the closest reason from the list.

## Start a study yourself

1. Tap **New study**.
2. Choose a material (or leave it), add at least one **teacher** and one **student**, and tick
   **Share contact details with their teacher** only for students who agreed.
3. When and where they meet ("Tuesdays, coffee shop"), planned lessons and a note are optional.
   The note is for practical things only, like "prefers mornings". Please keep personal matters out
   of the Hub. Only coordinators and staff can see it, and it stops at 280 characters.

**A teacher without an account is fine.** If you add one, you'll see "doesn't have an account yet".
Teachers do best with one, so they can update their own study. An admin can set one up: open the
teacher's page in **People** and choose **Give more access**. Until then, you keep their study up to
date.

## Keep a study up to date

Tap a study on **Studies**. From there you can:

- **Edit details**: the material, when and where, the lesson they're on, how many lessons are
  planned, how many times they've met. Every number is optional. A study with none shows "Active
  since March".
- **Pause** or **Resume**, or **Finish the study** when it's done.
- **Add a teacher or student**, or take someone out (what they finished stays on their record).
- **Finished**, beside a student, when they've completed the material.

Teachers with an account can do the progress, status and **Finished** parts themselves for their own
studies, and nothing else.

**Marked finished by mistake?** Open the **People** tab on the Bible studies page, search for their name, and under **Studies completed** tap **Undo**. (Staff can also do this from the person's page in People.) It comes off their record; we keep a note of who undid it and when.

## Past history

- **One at a time:** open the **People** tab on the Bible studies page, search for their name, then tap **Add a completed study**. (Staff can also use the Bible studies card on the person's page.)
  Choose the material (a retired one is fine), the date, and the teacher from People or by typing a
  name.
- **From a spreadsheet** (admins who cover every campus): **Materials**, then **Import past
  studies**. Choose a CSV with the columns `person_id` or `email`, `material`, `completed_on` and,
  if you like, `teacher`. You'll see a preview first: who was found, who wasn't and what's
  already there. Nobody is added to People; people are matched only by their People id or an email
  that belongs to exactly one person.

## Download

On **Studies**, tap **Download CSV** for the studies you're looking at (names only; no phone numbers
or emails).
