# The phone app (for staff)

**Who it's for:** staff who use the member app themselves and will help members get it.

## Two ways in, one app

- **The hub** is the staff tool you've been using: schedules, people, attendance, media.
- **The member app** is what the church family uses. It's the hub's web address followed by
  **/app** (for example, *your-hub-address*/app). You sign in with the same email and password.
- **The iPhone app** opens the same member app inside its own icon. It's being tested now and
  **isn't on the App Store yet**; it's coming. Until then, everyone uses the website version below.

## Get it on your phone (website version)

**iPhone:**
1. Open the member app address in **Safari** (not another browser).
2. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow pointing up).
3. Choose **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**.
4. Open it from the new icon. Turn on notifications from **More** → **Notifications**.

**Android:**
1. Open the member app address in Chrome.
2. Tap **Add to home screen** if the app offers it (on **More**, under **Get the app**), or open
   Chrome's menu (⋮) and choose **Install app** or **Add to Home screen**.

## Find your way around

Tabs along the bottom: **Home**, **Serve**, **Watch**, **Give** and **More**. (The church can change
these in the App Builder.)

- **Home**: a **Please reply** banner when someone has asked you to serve (tap **Accept** or **Can't make it** right on it), then **This week at …**:
  the **Next service**, where **You're serving** (with **Needs a reply** or **Accepted**), and
  events you're going to. Below that, the church's own cards, such as service times.
- **Serve**: your schedule. **Accept** or **Can't make it**, **Order of service**, **I'll be
  away…**, and your teams.
- **Watch**: the latest message and every series (see [Media](media-and-the-app.md)).
- **More**: **My info**, **Notifications**, **Chat**, **My groups**, **Tasks**, **Help**, **Send
  feedback**, light or dark mode, and **Sign out**. Staff and leaders also see **Staff dashboard**,
  which opens the hub.

**My info:** change your **Phone** and **Birthday** (tap **Save**), and your **Home address** (tap
**Save address**; it changes for your whole household). Your email is how you sign in, so the office
changes it: use **Tell the office** and **Send to the office** for that, or for family details.

**Going back:** on any screen deeper than a tab, tap **Back** at the top left. In the iPhone app you
can also swipe in from the left edge of the screen.

## What's different from the hub

- The app is for everyone; the hub is for staff and leaders. Scheduling other people, People,
  attendance, media and settings are only in the hub. Use **Staff dashboard** to get there.
- The bottom tabs are different (**Serve** instead of **Schedule**, plus **Watch** and **Give**).
- **The iPhone app:** sign in with your email code or password; Google sign-in isn't offered inside
  it. It doesn't show notifications yet ("Notifications aren't in the phone app yet. You'll still get
  emails."). Links to other websites, like maps, open in Safari.

## What to tell members

> Our church app is on the web at *[member app address]*. On an iPhone, open it in Safari, tap Share,
> then **Add to Home Screen**. On Android, open it in Chrome and choose **Install app** from the menu.
> Sign in with your email: we'll send you a code the first time. An App Store app is coming later.

Please don't tell members to look for it on the App Store yet.

## If something goes wrong

- **No notifications on iPhone:** they only work when the app was added to the Home Screen from
  Safari and opened from that icon (iPhone software 16.4 or newer).
- **"Notifications are blocked for this app":** allow them in the phone's settings, then come back.
- **"Can't reach … right now" in the iPhone app:** check the phone's connection and tap **Try
  again**. The first open after a quiet spell can take a few seconds.
- **A member can't sign in:** the church may only let invited people create accounts. Ask the office
  to add them.
