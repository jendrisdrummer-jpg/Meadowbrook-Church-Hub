# Take attendance on your phone

**Who it's for:** staff, leaders and anyone the office has given the Attendance switch. Made for a
phone in the room; works on any device. Everything saves as you tap; there's no Save button.

## Open the service

1. **Staff and leaders:** open **Attendance** (in **More** on a phone, under Services), then tap
   **Take attendance**. Or, on Home, tap **Attendance** beside a service.
   **Attendance-only accounts:** tap **Attendance** on the bottom bar.
2. You'll see recent services (today's first, with the campus in bold and a **Now** label on the one nearest the current time), with what's been recorded so far ("12 on the roll",
   "211 counted" or "Nothing yet").
3. Tap the service. For an older one, choose **Another date** and tap **Show services**.

## Roll call

1. Under **Take attendance**, tap each person you see. A tick appears and the green count at the top
   goes up ("5 here"). Tap again to undo.
2. **Families:** tap **Mark family** to mark everyone in that family at once. It changes to **All
   here**; tap it again to unmark them all.
3. **Find someone** searches by name. **Everyone**, **Here** and **Not yet** filter the list.
4. Children checked in at kids' check-in show **Checked in** and are already counted.

## New guests

1. Tap **New guest** (beside the filters).
2. Type their **First name** and, if you have them, last name, mobile phone and email.
3. Tap **Add and mark here**.
4. If we may already have them, you'll see **Is this the same person?** Tap **Yes, that's them**
   or **No, add someone new**.

People without staff or leader access only enter a name, and can add a child to a family with
**+ Child**.

## Headcounts

1. Scroll to the bottom and tap **Headcount** to open it. (Some campuses are set up to show the
   headcount first.)
2. Type the number for each room. It saves on its own and shows **Headcount saved**.
3. Only marked names, no count? **Use N from roll call** copies the roll call total into the first
   room. If a number is already there, you'll be asked first.

## Afterwards: the Attendance page

Open **Attendance** to see:

- Average, monthly average and highest Sunday, and a weekly chart (tap a week for its numbers).
  **Show** and **Service** change the time range and service time; **Download CSV** saves the
  numbers.
- **Each service**: one number per service. The headcount if one was entered, otherwise the names
  marked in roll call, plus kids checked in.
- **We've missed…**: regulars (here at least 3 times in the last 12 weeks) who've missed recent
  Sundays. Choose **2+ weeks**, **3+ weeks** or **4+ weeks**. A good list for a call or a note.
  Only Sundays when attendance was taken count. Home shows the same as "regulars have missed 2+
  Sundays".
- **First-time guests this month**.

## If something goes wrong

- **The service isn't listed:** the list shows the last two weeks and tomorrow. Use **Another date**.
- **You tapped the wrong person:** tap them again to untick.
- **A tap didn't stick (weak signal):** the tick is taken back and you'll see a message. Try again
  when you have a connection.
- **The same person appears twice:** mark one, and tell the office so they can merge the records.
- **Headcount is hard to find:** it's folded at the very bottom, below every family. Scroll all the
  way down.
