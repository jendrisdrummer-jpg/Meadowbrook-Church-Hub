# Media: Sunday videos in the app

**Who it's for:** staff who look after the Sunday message videos. Easiest on a computer. Videos come
in from the church's YouTube channel on their own, about every hour. Your job is to tidy the few
that need it. Nothing you do here changes anything on YouTube.

## The Library

1. Open **Media** (under Church app in the menu). A number on the menu means videos need a look.
2. Three chips across the top switch the list, each with a count:
   - **In the app**: what members can watch now.
   - **Not in the app**: left out because it's a Short, too short, has a skipped word in its
     title, isn't in the Sunday messages playlist, or someone hid it. Each says why.
   - **Needs a look**: the hub wasn't sure (for example it couldn't read the video's length, or it
     looks like a **Possible duplicate**). It turns amber until you open it.
3. Use **Every series** or the search to narrow the list. "Last checked …" under the list says when
   the hub last looked at YouTube. **Check for new videos** looks now.

## Sort out "Needs a look"

1. Tap **Needs a look**, then tap a video. An edit panel opens (the whole screen on a phone).
2. Check the title, date and details (below).
3. Tap **Show in app** to put it in the app, or **Hide from the app** to leave it out. Either one
   also keeps any changes you made.

To bring back something from **Not in the app**, open it and tap **Show in app**.

## Edit a video

Open any video and change what you need:

- **Title in the app** (the YouTube title is shown underneath for reference), **Date**,
  **Series** (pick one or type a new one), **Speaker**, **Scripture**, **Campus**, and **Notes
  under the video**.
- **Trim the start and end**: play to where the message should start, pause, and tap **Set to
  now** under **Starts at**. Do the same for **Ends at**, or type a time like 12:30. The app plays
  only that part.
- **Topics, picture and service**: topics members can tap to find more, your own picture, and
  which service it belongs to. **Fill in from the order of service** copies the speaker and title
  from that day's plan.

Tap **Save**. You'll see "Saved. The app shows it now." Closing with unsaved changes asks first.

**Add a video** (top of the page) takes a single YouTube address, for a special service or anything
that was left out.

## Setup (admins)

**Setup** (top of Media) is where the YouTube channel and rules live. Everyone on staff can read it;
only an admin who covers every campus can change it.

- **YouTube channel**: the channel address or @handle.
- **Sunday messages playlist**: paste a YouTube playlist address in **Only bring in videos from this
  playlist**, and only videos in that playlist come in. Leave it empty to bring in the whole
  channel. After setting it, Setup may offer to hide videos already in the app that aren't in the
  playlist; nothing is deleted.
- **Which videos stay out**: a minimum length (only used without a playlist) and **Skip videos whose
  title contains…**.
- **How your video titles are written**, and **Notices** (tell the app when we go live, or when a new
  message is added). Tap **Save**.

## What members see: the Watch tab

In the member app, **Watch** shows the **Latest message** at the top, **Search messages**, a tile for
each series, and **More messages**. When the church is streaming, a **Live** video sits at the top
instead. Only videos **In the app** appear, trimmed and titled as you set them.

## If something goes wrong

- **A video didn't come in:** tap **Check for new videos**. Still missing? Look under **Not in the
  app**, or use **Add a video**.
- **Wrong speaker or series:** the hub only fills these in when the video matches that day's order of
  service. Type them in and **Save**.
- **"This video isn't ready to play here yet":** YouTube may still be processing it. Try again later.
- **Setup says only an admin can change it:** ask an admin who covers both campuses.
