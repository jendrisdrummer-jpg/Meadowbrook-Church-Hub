# Staff beta: 15 things to try this week

**Who it's for:** every staff member in the beta. Try these at your own pace this week, on your
phone and on a computer if you can. Tick them off as you go.

**What we want to hear:** anything that felt wrong, slow, confusing or unkind, even small things.
"I didn't know what that button would do" is exactly the kind of feedback that helps.

**Please be careful with real people:** requests, reminders and announcements reach real people.
Practise scheduling on yourself or a test service, not on volunteers.

## How to tell us

**In the hub:** open **Feedback** (on a computer, the speech-bubble icon beside your name; on a
phone, **More** → **Feedback**). **In the app:** **More** → **Send feedback**.

1. Choose **What kind** (for example "Something isn't working" or "It works, but it's hard or
   slow").
2. Choose **How much does it matter?**: **Nice to have**, **Slows me down** or **Blocks Sunday**.
3. Write what you were doing, what you expected and what happened. The page notes where you were.
4. Tap **Send**.

Check **You said, we did** on the Feedback page to see what's changed. If something stops you on a
Sunday morning, also tell your contact in person.

Thank you for trying it first. Your patience now makes it easier for everyone after you.
