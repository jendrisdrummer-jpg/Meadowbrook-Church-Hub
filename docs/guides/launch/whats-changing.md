# What's changing and what isn't

*Draft FAQ for members, volunteers and parents. Replace the bracketed parts.*

## The short version

We're moving to one place for church life: the [church name] app and website. Some things are new.
Some things stay exactly the same for now. If you're unsure, ask. Nobody needs to rush.

## For everyone

**What is the new app?**
It is [church name] on your phone: service times, the latest message, giving, events, and your
serving schedule if you serve. It opens at **[app address]**.

**How do I get it?**
iPhone: open it in **Safari**, tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**. Android: open it in
Chrome, tap the menu, then **Install app** (or **Add to Home screen**).

**Is there an App Store app?**
Coming soon, but not yet. Use the steps above for now. It is the same app.

**How do I sign in?**
Type your email and tap **Email me a code**. Type the 6-digit code we send. On the website you can
also use **Sign in with Google**.

**Can I update my details?**
Yes. In the app, tap **More**, then **My info**. You can change your phone number and birthday.
Adults can also change the family's home address. To change an email address or other family
details, use **Tell the office** on the same page.

## For parents

**Is kids' check-in changing?**
**No. Nothing changes for parents yet.** Check in your children the same way you do today. If that
ever changes, we'll tell you well ahead, and we'll start with a small trial first.

## Giving

**Can I give through the new app?**
Yes. Tap **Give**.

**What about my recurring gift?**
Keep it where it is. Nothing happens to it for now. We'll let you know if that changes and what to
do. Please don't cancel it.

## For volunteers

**How will I hear about serving?**
When a leader asks you to serve, you get an email, and a notice in the app if you've turned
notifications on. Open it, then tap **Accept** or **Can't make it**. See the
[volunteer welcome](volunteer-welcome.md).

## Watch

**What will I find under Watch?**
The edited message from each Sunday, and past series. The full service video isn't posted there.
The latest message is at the top.

## Help

**Who do I ask?**
The welcome desk on Sunday, or [office contact]. In the app: **More**, then **Help** for short
how-to guides, or **Send feedback**.

**I can't sign in.**
Check that you're using the email address the church has for you. If you're new, the office may
need to add you first.
