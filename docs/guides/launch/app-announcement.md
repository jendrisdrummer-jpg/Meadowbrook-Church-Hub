## 1. Email or bulletin (short)

**Subject:** Our church app is ready for you

Hello, friends.

We have a new home for church life: the [church name] app. In it you can:

- see when and where we meet,
- watch the latest message,
- give,
- sign up for events,
- and, if you serve, see your schedule and answer requests.

**How to get it (about two minutes)**

*On an iPhone:*
1. Open **Safari** (the blue compass icon). Not another browser.
2. Type this address: **[app address]**
3. Tap the **Share** button (a square with an arrow pointing up).
4. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**, then tap **Add**.
5. Open the new [church name] icon on your screen.

*On an Android phone:*
1. Open **Chrome**.
2. Type this address: **[app address]**
3. Tap the **menu** (three dots, top right).
4. Tap **Install app** or **Add to Home screen**.

**Signing in:** type your email address and tap **Email me a code**. We will send you a
6-digit number. Type it in. If it doesn't arrive within a minute, check your spam folder. Please
use the email address the church already has for you.

**An App Store app is coming soon.** We will tell you when it is ready. You don't need to wait:
the steps above give you the same app.

**Nothing changes for kids' check-in.** Keep checking in your children the way you do now.

Need a hand? Ask at the welcome desk on Sunday, or contact [office contact]. We are glad to sit
with you and do it together.

[church name]
