# Welcome to the Hub

*One page for new staff at [church name]. Draft.*

The **Church Hub** is where we look after people, plan Sundays, schedule volunteers, take
attendance, chat, and look after the videos in the app. Members use the **member app** (the hub's
address followed by **/app**).

## Your first day

1. Ask the office for the hub's address and to add your email. Then sign in: **Email me a code**
   (or **Sign in with Google**). Choose a password if asked.
2. Read [Getting started](../getting-started.md) (10 minutes).
3. Get the member app on your own phone: [The phone app](../the-phone-app.md).
4. Look at **Help** (the ⓘ icon) for short how-tos.

## Then pick your guides

| If you... | Read |
|---|---|
| Schedule volunteers | [Schedule your team](../schedule-your-team.md) |
| Count on Sundays | [Take attendance](../take-attendance.md) |
| Look after videos | [Media and the app](../media-and-the-app.md) |
| Want a list of things to try | [Beta checklist](../beta-checklist.md) |

## Good to know

- **Kids' check-in is not on the hub yet.** Keep using what we use today. Nothing changes for
  parents.
- **Giving** is only seen by people with the **Finance** access. Ask if you need it.
- If you can't find a page, your account may not have that access yet. Ask the office.
- Something confusing or broken? Use **Feedback** in the hub. It really helps.
