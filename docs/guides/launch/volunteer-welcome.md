# Welcome, volunteers

*One page. Draft. Thank you for serving at [church name].*

## How you hear about serving

A team leader picks a Sunday and asks you to serve. You get an **email**. If you've turned on
notifications in the app, you get a notice too.

## Say yes or no

**From the email (easiest):** tap the link, then **Yes, I'll be there** or **I can't make it**.
You don't have to sign in.

**From the app:**
1. Open the app and tap **Serve** at the bottom.
2. Requests waiting for you are at the top, under **Please reply**.
3. Tap **Accept** if you can.
4. If you can't, tap **Can't make it**. Add a short reason if you like. Tap **Let my leader know**.

Saying no is fine. Your leader would rather know early, so they can find someone else. Tap
**Order of service** to see the plan for that day.

## Mark yourself away

On **Serve**, tap **I'll be away…**, choose your dates and tap **Save**. Leaders will see that you're
away and won't schedule you for those dates.

## Where to see your schedule

Tap **Serve**. Everything you've accepted and everything waiting for a reply is there. On **Home**,
the **This week** card also shows what you're serving.

## Notifications

In the app, tap **More**, then **Notifications**. On an iPhone, notifications only work if you
added the app to your Home Screen from Safari and open it from that icon. You'll still get emails
either way.

## Get the app

iPhone: Safari, **Share**, **Add to Home Screen**. Android: Chrome menu, **Install app**.
Address: **[app address]**. Sign in with your email and the code we send you. An App Store app is
coming soon.

## Questions

Ask your team leader or [office contact]. In the app: **More**, then **Help**.
