# Help desk replies

*Short, kind replies to copy and adjust. Use the person's first name. Never ask anyone for a
password.*

## I didn't get the code

> Hi [name], sorry about that. Codes can take a minute. Please check your spam or junk folder, then
> tap **Send a new code** on the sign-in screen. Make sure you typed the email address the church
> has for you. If it still doesn't come, tell us which address you used and we'll look.

## It says my email can't create an account

> Thank you for trying. That usually means we haven't added you yet, or we have a different email
> for you. Tell us which email you used and we'll sort it out.

## I can't find my kids

> Hi [name], don't worry. Kids' check-in hasn't changed, so it isn't in the new app yet. Please
> check in the way you do now. If you meant the family on your profile, tell us your name and
> we'll look into it.

## My family is wrong

> Thanks for telling us. In the app, tap **More**, then **My info**. You can update your phone,
> birthday and home address there. For other family changes, tap **Tell the office**, say what's
> wrong, and tap **Send to the office**. We'll fix it and let you know.

## How do I stop notifications

> In the app, tap **More**, then **Notifications**, where you can choose which notices you get. To
> stop the pop-ups on your phone, you can also turn them off in your phone's settings. We'd like to
> still reach you about serving, so tell us if you'd rather not hear from us at all.

## How do I get the app

> Hi [name]. iPhone: open **[app address]** in Safari, tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**.
> Android: open it in Chrome, tap the menu, then **Install app**. An App Store version is coming
> soon.

## I'm looking for the App Store app

> It's coming soon, but isn't there yet. For now, open **[app address]** in Safari, tap **Share**,
> then **Add to Home Screen**. It's the same app.

## What about my recurring gift

> Thank you for giving. Please leave it as it is. We'll let you know if anything changes.

## I'm not getting serving requests

> Thanks for letting us know. Please check your spam folder and that we have the right email. Your
> team leader can check you're on the team. In the app, tap **Serve** to see requests waiting.
