# Schedule your team

**Who it's for:** staff and team leaders who schedule volunteers. Easiest on a computer; works on a
phone. **Nobody hears anything until you tap Send requests**, so you can take your time.

## Plan a month: Volunteer schedule

1. Open **Volunteer schedule** (under Services in the menu).
2. Use the arrows or **This month** to pick the month. The pill beside the month shows how many
   open spots there are.
3. Under **Team**, pick your team. The grid shows each Sunday across the top and each position down
   the side. Your team's people appear in a **Team** list on the right, with how many times they
   serve this month and any dates they're away.
4. To place someone: tap their name in the **Team** list, then tap an **Open** spot (spots they
   can take show their name). On a computer you can also drag a name onto a spot. Or tap **Open**
   on its own to pick from a list, best choices first.
5. To fill the whole month at once, tap **Fill the month**, then **Fill open spots**. It only uses
   people assigned to each position, skips anyone away or already serving then, and spreads Sundays
   out fairly. Look it over afterwards.
6. Tap a name in the grid to see their reply or remove them.
7. When you're happy, tap **Send N requests** (top of the page), check the list, then tap
   **Send to N people**. Each person gets one email and app notification with all their spots.

## Fill one service: Who's serving

1. Open **Services**, tap the service, then the **Who's serving** tab. (From Home, the **Schedule**
   button on a service takes you straight here.)
2. Pick your team in the **Team** box, or leave **All teams**.
3. Tap **Schedule someone** next to an open position and pick a person. Labels warn you if someone
   is away, already serving at that time, or doesn't usually serve at this campus; you'll be asked
   before you **Schedule anyway**.
4. Short on time? **Fill open spots** beside a team fills that team's gaps.
5. People you add show **Not asked yet**. Tap **Send N requests**, check the list, then send.

## What the labels mean

- **Not asked yet**: added, but no request sent. **Waiting**: asked, no reply yet.
  **Accepted** and **Declined** (with their reason underneath, if they gave one).
- **Can't be reached**: this person has no email or account, or has turned notices off. Tap the
  label to see which. Requests won't reach them, so let them know yourself, or ask the office to add
  an email. The **Send** list also warns you before you send.

## When someone can't make it

1. You'll get a notice. On Home, a **declined spots to cover** tile appears under **Needs your
   attention**; tapping it opens the service.
2. On the service's **Who's serving** tab, the person shows **Declined**.
3. Tap **Find a replacement** beside them and pick someone. They're added as **Not asked yet**.
4. Tap **Send 1 request**.

To nudge people who haven't answered, tap **Remind N who haven't replied**, then **Send reminder**.
Anyone reminded in the last 12 hours is skipped, so it's safe to tap again later.

## What volunteers see

They get an email and (if they've turned them on) a notification.

- **From the email:** they tap the link, then **Yes, I'll be there** or **I can't make it**. They
  don't need to sign in.
- **In the hub or app:** **My Schedule** (the **Serve** tab in the app) lists requests under
  **Please reply**. They tap **Accept**, or **Can't make it**, add a reason if they like, and tap
  **Let my leader know**.
- **Away dates:** on the same page, **I'll be away…** stops them being scheduled on those days.

Try it on yourself: schedule yourself on a test service, send the request, and answer it from your
email and from **My Schedule**.

## If something goes wrong

- **No one in the pick list:** nobody is assigned to that position yet. Tap **Show everyone on the
  team**, or **Someone not on the team…**, or tick people for the position on the team's page.
- **"No one available to fill the open spots":** check who's on the team, who's away, and who
  serves at this campus.
- **Someone serves at both campuses but isn't offered:** ask an admin to add their serving campuses.
- **The month grid lets you place people on Sundays that have already passed.** Check the date at
  the top of the column before you place someone.
