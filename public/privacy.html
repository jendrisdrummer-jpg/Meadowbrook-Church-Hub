Privacy

Meadowbrook Church Hub · Last updated October 2026

Meadowbrook Church Hub is the system Meadowbrook Church uses to plan services, schedule volunteers, take attendance and check children in. This page explains what it stores and how it is used.

Signing in

You sign in with your Google account. The hub receives only your name, email address and profile picture from Google. It never sees your Google password, and it cannot read your email, files or anything else in your Google account.

What we store

  • Contact details: name, email, phone, address and household.
  • Church information, such as campus, membership status, the teams you serve on, your serving schedule and dates you're away.
  • Attendance and children's check-in records.
  • For children: birthdate, school grade, allergies and medical notes, and the adults allowed to pick them up, so leaders can keep them safe.
  • Photos, if the church adds one.

Who can see it

Only church staff and leaders who need it to serve you. Volunteers see their own schedule. Children's medical and allergy details are shown to the leaders caring for them.

What we never do

We do not sell, rent or share your information with advertisers or anyone outside the church. It is not used for marketing.

Where it's kept

The hub runs on a secure cloud server in the United States. It is protected by encrypted connections (HTTPS) and backed up daily.

Your choices

You can ask the church office to show you, correct or delete your information at any time.
